# Dashboard and Monitoring

The **dashboard** is the real-time monitoring center. It shows all cameras in a grid with
live previews, quick access to details and the people detection panel.

## Camera grid

The dashboard shows cameras as **tiles** arranged in a grid:

- Each tile displays the camera **name**.
- The video is a real-time preview.
- Click a tile to open the **Camera Details** in its own window.

### Moving and resizing

- **Drag** a tile to rearrange the camera position in the grid.
- Use the **resize** icon (bottom-right corner) to change the tile size.

<Callout variant="info" title="Saved positions">
Tile positions and sizes are saved automatically and restored on the next session.
</Callout>

## Detection panel

In the bottom-right corner of the dashboard is the **Detection** panel (person icon):

- Shows the number of **detected** people on the cameras.
- With a camera selected, shows the **people count** and the detection **masks**.
- **Select All** — shows the total of all cameras.
- **Ban Person** — starts the person ban flow.
- **Sign Out** — ends the session on the current profile.

## Camera details (window)

When you open a camera, a window presents:

| Section | Function |
| --- | --- |
| **Area Detection** | Live view with people count in the area. |
| **Face Recognition** | Known/unknown face detection, face search. |
| **Behavior Analysis** | Suspicious behavior alerts. |
| **Information** | Camera details (name, location, connection type, task). |
| **Edit** | Change name and location. |
| **Remove** | Delete the camera. |

<Callout variant="success" title="Real-time verification">
Detections are presented as soon as they are processed by the server, with images of the
detection moment.
</Callout>
