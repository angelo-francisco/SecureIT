# Area Detection

The **Area Detection** task uses the YOLO model (YOLOv11) to detect and count people in
real time in the area monitored by the camera.

## How it works

1. The video is analyzed at a rate controlled by the [settings](/docs/en/settings)
   (frames per second and detection frequency).
2. Each detected person is counted and the information is sent to the server.
3. The **detection panel** on the dashboard shows the total and per-camera count.

## People count

The detection panel shows:

- **Total count** of all selected cameras.
- **Per-camera count** when a camera is selected.
- Indicator of active detection **masks**.

### Count alerts

SecureIT generates a count alert when the number of detected people in the area
**exceeds the configured value** during the monitoring period. The alert includes an
image of the detection moment.

## Monitoring schedule

Area detection only works during the **monitoring schedule** defined in the
[settings](/docs/en/settings) (start and end time). Outside this schedule, monitoring is
paused.

## Interaction with other tasks

Area detection shares the same camera with face recognition: detected faces are processed
by recognition, but the people count is independent.

<Callout variant="info" title="AI model">
People detection uses the **YOLOv11** model. The model runs locally on the server where
SecureIT is installed — the video is not sent to external services.
</Callout>
