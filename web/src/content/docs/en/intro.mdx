# Welcome to SecureIT

**SecureIT** is a smart, cross-platform surveillance system that turns regular cameras
into an AI-powered security hub. Connect your cameras, register people and receive
real-time alerts — on your computer, browser or phone.

## What SecureIT does

SecureIT monitors cameras in real time and uses AI models to:

- **Detect people** — counts and flags people in the monitored area, with configurable
  monitoring hours.
- **Recognize faces** — identifies known and unknown people from the photos you register
  in the **People** section.
- **Analyze behavior** — detects suspicious movement, bladed weapons, possible theft and
  robbery or escape situations.
- **Alert in real time** — generates notifications with a snapshot of the moment, with
  several priority levels.
