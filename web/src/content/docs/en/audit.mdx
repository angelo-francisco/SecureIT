# Audit Logs

**Audit Logs** keep the history of the actions performed in SecureIT. This feature is
useful to understand who did what and when — a basis for accountability and compliance.

## What is recorded

Audit logs include information such as:

- **Date and time** of the action.
- **User** who performed the action.
- **Action** executed.
- **Target** of the action (camera, person, setting, etc.).

Typical recorded actions include creating/editing/removing cameras, registering/banning
people, changing settings and activating/revoking licenses.

<Callout variant="info" title="Viewing logs">
Logs can be viewed through the **audit** menu in the app. For very old actions, the logs
can be exported for external review.
</Callout>
