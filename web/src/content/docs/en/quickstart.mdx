# Getting Started

This guide walks you through the initial SecureIT setup, from the first login to your
first camera streaming.

## 1. Sign in

Open the SecureIT app on your computer. The first time you open it, the **Sign In** screen
appears.

1. Enter your **email address**.
2. Enter your **password**.
3. Click **Next**.

<Callout variant="warning" title="No account yet?">
Click "Create Account" on the sign-in screen to be redirected to the registration page on
the website. You can also use the **email code** flow to sign in without a password.
</Callout>

## 2. Choose a profile

After signing in, SecureIT shows the **profiles** available on your account
(e.g. "Home", "Office"). Each profile has its own cameras, people and settings.

- Click a profile to enter.
- If the profile has a **PIN**, the app asks for the code before continuing.
- Use **My Account** to manage profiles and account security.

## 3. Activate the license

SecureIT only works with an active license. If you have not activated yet:

1. Go to **Others → License** (inside Settings).
2. Enter the key in the `SEC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX` format.
3. Click **Activate License**.

<Callout variant="info" title="Buy a license">
To purchase a license, visit the [Licenses](/pricing) page on the website or **My Account**,
choose a plan and follow the payment process.
</Callout>

## 4. Register your first camera

1. In the bottom menu, click **Cameras**.
2. Click **New Camera**.
3. Give it a **name** and a **location** (e.g. "Main entrance", "Home").
4. Choose the **connection type** (Local/USB, Wi-Fi or demo video file).
5. Choose the camera **task**: Area Detection, Face Recognition or Behavior Analysis.
6. Click **Add**.

The camera appears on the dashboard and starts streaming automatically.

<Callout variant="success" title="Tip">
Use the **Identify** button in the camera list to automatically detect cameras physically
connected to the computer.
</Callout>
