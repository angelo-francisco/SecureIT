# Frequently Asked Questions

## Does SecureIT work with any camera?

SecureIT supports **physically connected** cameras (USB/Webcam) and **network cameras**
reachable by HTTP, HTTPS or RTSP. It also allows using a **video file** for demonstration.

## Where is the video processed?

All processing is done **locally** on the server where SecureIT is installed. The video
and data are not sent to external AI services.

## Do I need a license to use SecureIT?

Yes. Each profile needs an **active license** to work. The license determines the
available features (face recognition, behavior analysis) and the profile limits
(e.g. number of cameras).

## How do I activate my license?

Go to **Settings → License**, enter the key in the `SEC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX` format and
click **Activate License**. You can buy a license on the [Licenses](/pricing) page.

## How do I stop a license from working?

The license can be **revoked** from the settings. After revocation, limited features stay
blocked until a new activation.

## How do I identify who appears on the cameras?

1. Register the person in the [People](/docs/en/people) section with a clear photo.
2. Configure the camera with the **Face Recognition** (`FR`) task.
3. When the person appears, SecureIT identifies them automatically.

## I am not receiving notifications. What should I check?

- Confirm the **monitoring schedule** is active (detections only occur within the schedule
  defined in the [settings](/docs/en/settings)).
- Check the **alert cooldown** — repeated notifications of the same person may be
  suppressed.
- Confirm the **license** includes the feature in question.

## Does face recognition work at night?

Recognition depends on image quality. In low-light conditions, accuracy may decrease. Use
cameras with good night capture when monitoring at night.

## Can I use the same profile on more than one computer?

The account and profiles can be used on more than one device. The license is synced
periodically (every 6 hours) with the server.

## How do I separate "Home" from "Office"?

Use **profiles**. Each profile has its own cameras, people and settings. See the
[Profiles and PIN](/docs/en/profiles) page.
